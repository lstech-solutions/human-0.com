\subsection{Manifesto}

Humanity is simultaneously running two non-linear processes: an accelerating planetary crisis and an expanding digital infrastructure largely indifferent to physical limits. The systems that secure our digital assets today---hashpower, stake, and centralized identity---do not secure our future on this planet.

HUMAN-ZERO begins from a simple assertion: sustainability must become a \textbf{first-class cryptographic primitive}. Our digital identities should not only prove ownership or solvency, but also attest to our contribution to planetary stability. We call this primitive \textbf{Proof of Sustainable Humanity (PoSH)}.

\subsubsection{Traditional Consensus}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Proof of Work} consumes energy as a Sybil-resistance mechanism; its security budget is paid in thermodynamic cost.
\item \textbf{Proof of Stake} concentrates control in capital and remains structurally agnostic to real-world emissions or ecological impact.
\end{itemize}

Neither model encodes a notion of \textit{human-scale, positive environmental action}. They secure ledgers; they do not secure the biosphere.

\subsubsection{The PoSH Approach}

HUMAN-ZERO proposes a complementary axis of trust: a ledger in which each entry corresponds to a verifiable sustainability event attributed to a unique human. Measurement, reporting, and verification (MRV) pipelines generate signed records of renewable energy use, low-carbon mobility, or regenerative actions.

At the core of PoSH is a simple mapping:

\begin{equation}
\text{Human} \rightarrow \mathsf{humanId} \rightarrow \{\text{impact commitments on-chain}\}
\end{equation}

Each commitment is an immutable binding between a pseudonymous human identifier and an attested sustainability event.

\subsection{Proof of Sustainable Humanity (PoSH)}

\subsubsection{Introduction}

The accelerating climate crisis reveals a structural gap in how digital systems recognize, value, and verify human contributions to environmental sustainability. Existing blockchain consensus mechanisms---such as Proof of Work (PoW) and Proof of Stake (PoS)---are fundamentally unsuitable for representing or incentivizing human-scale sustainable action.

\textbf{Proof of Sustainable Humanity (PoSH)} is introduced as a new primitive: a cryptographically verifiable, privacy-preserving mechanism that represents the positive, measurable environmental actions performed by unique human individuals.

\subsubsection{Design Principles}

PoSH is built upon the following principles:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Human-centeredness:} The unit of verification is a unique human, not a machine or a capital pool.
\item \textbf{Environmental positivity:} Each proof must correspond to a measurable positive environmental action.
\item \textbf{Non-extractiveness:} Minting a PoSH proof must require no protocol-level fee beyond blockchain gas costs.
\item \textbf{Privacy preservation:} Raw sustainability event data remains off-chain and may be wrapped in zero-knowledge proofs.
\item \textbf{Transparency and verifiability:} All claims must be backed by auditable, cryptographically signed MRV records.
\item \textbf{Global accessibility:} The protocol must be usable regardless of geographical location, financial resources, or device constraints.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{System Architecture}

The PoSH system is composed of three primary layers:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Identity Layer} - Modular, provider-agnostic identity architecture
\item \textbf{Impact Verification Layer} - MRV adapters and oracle networks
\item \textbf{On-chain Proof Registration} - Immutable proof registry and soulbound tokens
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Identity Layer}

Let $\textsf{IdP}$ denote any identity provider---such as Humanity Protocol, World ID, Sismo, BrightID, Proof of Humanity, or any W3C-compatible verifiable credential system. Each provider outputs a proof of uniqueness.

PoSH abstracts these systems under a unified commitment:

\begin{equation}
\mathsf{humanId} = H(\textsf{IdP.output} \parallel \textsf{salt})
\end{equation}

This ensures unlinkability across platforms while permitting the user to switch identity providers without invalidating past PoSH proofs.

\paragraph{Impact Verification Layer}

Each sustainability action is captured by MRV adapters connected to real-world data sources:

\begin{itemize}
\item Smart meters and renewable energy certificates (I-RECs)
\item Electric vehicle (EV) charging systems
\item Solar generation and community microgrids
\item Low-carbon mobility data
\item Recycling and waste management systems
\end{itemize}

The MRV process produces a digitally signed report containing:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Action type} (e.g., renewable energy generation)
\item \textbf{Impact value} (e.g., 100 kWh)
\item \textbf{Carbon equivalent} ($\text{CO}_2\text{e}$)
\item \textbf{Timestamp} and \textbf{location} (optional, privacy-preserving)
\item \textbf{Methodology reference} (e.g., I-REC standard)
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Oracle Verification}

Oracle nodes perform four checks:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Data authenticity} - Verify digital signatures
\item \textbf{No double counting} - Check for duplicate submissions
\item \textbf{External validation} - Cross-reference with trusted data sources
\item \textbf{Plausibility} - Assess logical consistency of reported values
\end{enumerate}

Once validated, the oracle generates a signed claim:

\begin{equation}
\mathsf{claim} = H(\mathsf{humanId} \parallel \mathsf{mrvHash} \parallel \mathsf{impactValue} \parallel \mathsf{timestamp})
\end{equation}

\subsubsection{On-Chain Proof Registration}

The \textbf{PoSH Proof Registry} contract stores canonical, deduplicated proof records. Each record contains:

\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathsf{humanId}$ - Pseudonymous human identifier
\item $\mathsf{RWAP}$ - Real-World Action Proof commitment
\item $\mathsf{impactValue}$ - Quantified environmental impact
\item $\mathsf{timestamp}$ - When the action occurred
\item $\mathsf{oracleSignature}$ - Cryptographic attestation
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Zero-Knowledge Privacy}

PoSH supports optional zero-knowledge proofs (ZKPs) to ensure privacy while maintaining verifiability. A user can prove:

\begin{equation}
\text{"I am a unique human with } \geq X \text{ carbon-equivalent impact"}
\end{equation}

without revealing meter IDs, timestamps, or location.

\subsubsection{Security Properties}

We outline the core security properties:

\textbf{Completeness:} If MRV data is correct and oracle signatures are valid, then a valid PoSH proof is always accepted.

\textbf{Soundness:} No adversary can forge a PoSH proof without valid MRV and oracle signatures.

\textbf{Privacy:} Zero-knowledge proofs ensure verifiers learn only exposed aggregates, not raw MRV data.

\textbf{Non-transferability:} PoSH proofs are soulbound; they cannot be transferred between identities.

\textbf{Sybil resistance:} Mitigated via unique human proofs, rate limiting, and oracle multisignature requirements.

\subsection{Conclusion}

PoSH is proposed as a foundational cryptographic primitive for representing human-scale sustainability contributions. By integrating MRV, decentralized oracles, privacy-preserving cryptography, and transparent on-chain registries, PoSH enables a future where positive environmental actions can be verified, recognized, and composed into digital identity systems without compromising individual privacy or equity.

Zero is not an absence; it is a boundary condition. Net zero is not a slogan, but a statement that can---and must---be proven. HUMAN-ZERO is an invitation to treat that proof as a public good.
